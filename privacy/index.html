Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Ox Jobs ("Blue Ox Jobs," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our platform, which matches interns with startups through "Intern Pods" (the "Service"). It applies to interns, startups, administrators, and anyone else who visits or creates an account on the Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign up, authentication is handled by our identity provider, Clerk, using Google sign-in or email. We receive and store:

  • Your name and email address
  • A unique account identifier issued by Clerk
  • Your assigned role on the platform: intern, startup, or admin

1.2 Intern Profile Information

If you use the Service as an intern, we collect the information you provide in your profile, which may include:

  • Location and timezone
  • WhatsApp number
  • Main skill and detailed skills description
  • Links to your GitHub, portfolio, and LinkedIn profiles
  • Availability and preferred start date
  • English proficiency level
  • A description of projects you have worked on

1.3 Startup Profile Information

If you use the Service as a startup, we collect the information you provide about your company, which may include:

  • Company name, website, and description
  • Industry and team size
  • Location, contact email, and phone number
  • The roles you are looking to fill

1.4 Pod Selection Records

When a startup selects an intern to join a Pod, we record which startup selected which intern, and whether the placement has been confirmed. This information is visible to the startup that made the selection, the intern involved, and administrators.

1.5 Information Collected Automatically

Our authentication provider and hosting infrastructure automatically process technical information necessary to operate the Service, such as session tokens, authentication cookies, IP address, and browser/device information used for security and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your identity
  • Display intern profiles to startups browsing for candidates, and display startup profiles to admins for approval
  • Match interns with startups and manage Pod placements
  • Review and moderate intern and startup profiles before they go live on the platform
  • Communicate with you about your account, your placement, or your use of the Service
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Service
  • Comply with our legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases: performance of a contract with you (operating your account and facilitating Pod placements), our legitimate interests (operating, securing, and improving the Service), and your consent where applicable (such as optional profile fields you choose to provide).

5. Cookies

The Service uses cookies set by our authentication provider, Clerk, to keep you signed in and to maintain a secure session. These cookies are essential to the operation of the Service. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to comply with a legal obligation, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Blue Ox Jobs operates across the EU, US, and Gulf regions, and our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect information transferred internationally.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at obed@blueoxkampus.com.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the Service. Your continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
